# Code of Conduct

liftlog is a friendly place for people who care about training well and sharing what they know.

## Our standard

* Be kind and respectful. Disagree about technique and code, not about people.
* Assume good intent, and explain your reasoning with sources where you can.
* No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or body-shaming.
* No spam, no promotion of supplements or paid programs in issues, pull requests or exercise data.
* Keep health claims modest: this project is a logbook and a learning aid, not medical advice.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space related to the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on the repository page and describe the situation, or contact a maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This code is adapted from the ideas of the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
